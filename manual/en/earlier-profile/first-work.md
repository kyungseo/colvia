# Extra exercise · Review a booking policy update

[User guide](README.md)

Try this policy: customers can reschedule online at least 24 hours before a visit and must contact support closer to the visit. Fees are undecided. Ask the agent to draft a FAQ entry and a support reply. Once the agreed edits and checks are done, the agent shows you what changed in the FAQ and the support reply. Read what each would tell a customer.

> My visit is exactly 24 hours away. Can I reschedule on the booking page? What if it’s only 23 hours away?

At 24 hours, both drafts should direct the customer to the booking page. At 23 hours, both should direct them to support. Check the fee wording too: the policy leaves that decision open. If either draft promises a free change, ask the agent to correct that sentence and show you both drafts again.

Satisfied? Say “I accept these drafts.” Your decision belongs to the drafts you reviewed. You still choose when to show the new wording to customers.
